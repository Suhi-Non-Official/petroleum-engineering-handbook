# Petroleum Engineering Tools & Unit Converter Specifications

## Supported Tools

### 1. Unit Converter
Supports 13 unit categories: Pressure, Temperature, Length, Area, Volume, Flow Rate, Mass, Density, Viscosity, Permeability, Energy, Power, Time.

### 2. Darcy Radial Flow Rate Calculator
- **Formula**: \(q = \frac{0.00708 k h \Delta P}{\mu B \ln(r_e/r_w)}\)
- **Source**: Petroleum Engineering Handbook Vol 1 & Vol 5

### 3. Hydrostatic Pressure Calculator
- **Formula**: \(P = 0.052 \times MW \times TVD\)
- **Source**: Petroleum Engineering Handbook Vol 2

### 4. Productivity Index Calculator
- **Formula**: \(J = \frac{q}{P_e - P_{wf}}\)
- **Source**: Petroleum Engineering Handbook Vol 4

### 5. API Gravity Converter
- **Formula**: \(\text{API} = \frac{141.5}{\text{SG}} - 131.5\)
- **Source**: Petroleum Engineering Handbook Vol 1
